`box.hpp`, `stream.hpp` and `sharedbox_c.h`: box layout 3.2 and stream
layout 1.4 hold metadata after the record or the description table.
`handle` and `stream` gain `metadata_head` and `read_metadata`, `handle` and
`stream_sender` gain `add_metadata`, `handle` gains
`write(values, doc, lock_timeout)`, which writes fields and adds a version in
one write, `stream_sender` gains `can_add_metadata`, `stream_reader` gains
`metadata_version_at`, and `sharedbox_c.h` adds `sbx_metadata_head`,
`sbx_read_metadata`, `sbx_add_metadata` and `SBX_E_METADATA_FULL`.
