`SharedStream.metadata`: a stream keeps metadata the same way.
`SharedStream.create(Reading, capacity=8, metadata={"unit": "kg"})` stores
version 1, and `SharedSendStream.add_metadata` adds a version that applies
from the next item sent. `SharedReceiveStream.metadata` and
`metadata_version` give the version of the item the reader received last.
`SharedStream.create` takes `metadata_capacity` and `metadata_versions`, and
`create` and `attach` take `metadata_type`. `sharedbox` 0.7.0 and earlier
cannot open a stream created with metadata or a `metadata_capacity`.
