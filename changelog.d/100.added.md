`Call`, `RequestFuture`, `RequestServer` and `call`: a box field declared as
`move: Call[[float], float] = call()` carries a typed request from
any process to the one process that serves it. `box.move.serve(handler)`
answers with a sync or async handler, and `box.move(5.0)` returns a
`RequestFuture` waited on with `.result(timeout)` or `await`. Of the requests
sent while the handler runs, only the newest is handled next. `RequestError`,
`RequestSupersededError` and `ServerExitedError` report a failed, replaced or
abandoned request, and `AlreadyServedError` a second server. Call fields are
kind 76 of box layout 3.3, and `box.hpp` adds the call protocol steps on
`handle` for C++.
