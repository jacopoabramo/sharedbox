`Call`, `CallFuture` and `CallServer`: a box field declared as
`move: Call[[float], float] = field(init=False)` carries a typed request from
any process to the one process that serves it. `box.move.serve(handler)`
answers with a sync or async handler, and `box.move(5.0)` returns a
`CallFuture` waited on with `.result(timeout)` or `await`. Of the requests sent
while the handler runs, only the newest is handled next. `CallError`,
`CallSupersededError`, `ServerExitedError` and `CallServedError` report a
failed, replaced or unserved call. Call fields are kind 76 of box layout 3.3,
and `box.hpp` adds the call protocol steps on `handle` for C++.
