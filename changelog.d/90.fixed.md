The `atexit` hook of `sharedbox` closes every box and stream the process
still has open and releases the native objects of `SharedBox` subclasses,
their watches and events groups, so nanobind reports no leaked instances at
exit when a box class defined at module level outlives shutdown. A box or
stream used after the hook raises `BoxClosedError` or `StreamClosedError`,
and creating or attaching a box raises `BoxClosedError`.
